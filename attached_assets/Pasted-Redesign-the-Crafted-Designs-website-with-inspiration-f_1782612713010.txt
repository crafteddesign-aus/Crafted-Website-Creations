Redesign the Crafted Designs website with inspiration from the bold,
premium and results-focused presentation used by leading Australian
digital agencies.

Do not directly copy another website's layout, graphics, text or branding.
Use the inspiration only to improve hierarchy, confidence, movement,
project presentation and conversion.

BRAND DIRECTION

Keep the existing Crafted Designs black, charcoal and orange identity.

Use:
- Main background: #0D0D0D
- Card background: #151515
- Warm light background: #F2EFE9
- White text: #FAFAFA
- Dark text: #161616
- Orange accent: #FF8C00
- Muted text: #A6A6A6
- Subtle borders: rgba(255,255,255,0.12)

The overall style should feel:
- Premium
- Bold
- Modern
- Confident
- Approachable
- Results-focused
- Suitable for local Whitsundays businesses

TYPOGRAPHY

Use a bold modern sans-serif such as Manrope or Sora for the main
headings.

Use Cormorant Garamond sparingly for selected italic words such as
"crafted", "better" or "growth".

Use Nunito Sans or Inter for body copy.

Use large typography:
- Hero heading: clamp(3rem, 7vw, 6rem)
- Section headings: clamp(2.4rem, 5vw, 4.5rem)
- Body text: 18px to 20px
- Generous line height and white space

ANIMATION

Use subtle GSAP, Motion or CSS animations only where appropriate:

- Fade and rise sections as they enter the viewport
- Slow floating website mock-ups
- Very subtle image zoom on hover
- Moving arrows inside buttons
- Smooth marquee strip
- Count-up animation only when genuine statistics are available

Animations must:
- Remain smooth on mobile
- Respect prefers-reduced-motion
- Never delay access to content
- Avoid excessive spinning, bouncing or parallax

MOBILE

The mobile version is equally important.

Ensure:
- Hero remains easy to understand
- Mock-ups stack underneath the text
- Buttons are full-width where necessary
- Text does not become oversized
- Project screenshots remain readable
- No horizontal overflow
- Tap targets are at least 44px high
- Navigation uses a polished full-screen menu
